# Dum town stores (level 0). At most 8.
#
# name / owner: shown in the shop window.
# sign:  item whose picture is painted on the shop door.
# items: what the store sells (it never runs out). Prices are the item 'value' in items.txt.
# buys:  kinds of items the store buys, for half their value:
#        weapon armor helm ring amulet light food potion scroll fuel wand rod, and 'staff' (weapons
#        with spell power).
#        Every store buys quest treasures for their full value.
