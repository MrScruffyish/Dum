# Dum spell definitions.
#
# type:    bolt (projectile at auto-aimed target), ball (projectile that explodes, radius),
#          nova (burst around the caster), heal, bless, light, detect, blink, map,
#          recall (after 'duration' + 0..'range' seconds: dungeon -> town, town -> deepest level),
#          teleport (far away, at least 'range' tiles if possible), haste (move/attack/cast faster),
#          shield (+8 armor class), resist (fire, cold, poison for you and allies in 'radius'),
#          dig (turns the wall or rubble in front of you, up to 'range' tiles, into floor),
#          feed (you are full), whirlwind (a melee blow against everything around you),
#          berserk (+4 to hit, +3 damage, -2 armor class, heals 'dice'),
#          descent (after 'duration' seconds the party falls 'range' levels deeper; endless dungeon only)
# effect:  sleep, fear, slow or teleport (sends it far away) - extra effect of a bolt/ball/nova on monsters (bosses are
#          immune, deep monsters often resist). A spell with no 'dice' does no damage.
# recharge: seconds before the spell can be used again (abilities use this instead of mana).
# ability = yes: a fighting technique (warriors), shown as such and costs no mana.
# target = self: heal only the caster (otherwise the most wounded ally you look at).
# element: physical, arcane, fire, cold (slows), holy (x2 vs undead), poison
# dice:    base damage/healing, plus 'scale' per caster level
# Damage/healing = dice + scale * level + spell power,
# spell power = class magic skill / 2 + cast stat modifier + staff bonus.
